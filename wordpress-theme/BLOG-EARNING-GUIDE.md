# Kadvia Blog: kaise chalayein aur kaise kamayein

Yeh guide simple Hinglish mein hai. Ise step by step follow karo.

---

## 1. Blog website par kahan hai

- Menu mein **Blog** ka link hai: `kadvia.com/blog/`
- Theme upload karte hi **6 starter articles** apne aap ban jaate hain, cover image ke saath:
  1. What is appointment setting?
  2. Cold email that lands in the inbox: 9 rules
  3. LinkedIn outreach that gets replies
  4. In-house SDR or outsourced appointment setting?
  5. Write your ideal customer profile in 30 minutes
  6. Follow-up messages: how many, how often, what to say
- Home page par "From the blog" section mein latest 3 articles khud dikhte hain.
- Har article ke end mein **"Book a strategy call"** ka box hai. Isse blog padhne wale aapke client ban sakte hain.

## 2. Naya article kaise likhein

1. wp-admin mein **Posts → Add New** par jao.
2. **Title** likho, jaise "How to get meetings with CFOs in India".
3. Neeche content likho. Headings ke liye **Heading (H2)** block use karo.
4. Right side mein yeh bharo:
   - **Category:** Cold Email, LinkedIn, Sales Strategy ya Appointment Setting
   - **Featured image:** 1200 x 630 size ki image. Canva mein "Blog banner" template se bana sakte ho.
   - **Excerpt:** 1–2 line ka summary. Yeh blog card par dikhta hai.
5. **Publish** dabao. Article Blog page aur Home page dono par aa jaayega.

Starter articles ko aap edit ya delete kar sakte ho: **Posts** mein jao, phir Edit ya Trash karo.

## 3. Blog se kamai ke 5 tareeke

Kadvia jaisi service website ke liye **pehla tareeka sabse bada hai**.

### A. Apne clients laana (sabse zyada paisa)
- Log Google par "how to book B2B meetings" jaisi cheezein search karte hain, aapka article padhte hain, aur end mein "Book a strategy call" dabate hain.
- Ek client se jo kamai hoti hai, woh ads ki hazaaron views se zyada ho sakti hai.
- Har article ko LinkedIn par bhi share karo. Har article ke neeche share button hai.

### B. Google AdSense (ads se kamai)
1. Blog par kam se kam **15–20 achhe, original articles** likho.
2. In pages ka hona zaroori hai: About, Contact aur **Privacy Policy**. Privacy Policy banane ke liye **Settings → Privacy** mein jao.
3. **adsense.google.com** par apply karo. Google approve karne mein kuch din se kuch hafte lagata hai.
4. Approval ke baad ad unit ka code copy karo.
5. **Appearance → Customize → Kadvia Blog and Ads → Ad code** mein paste karke **Publish** karo.
   Ad har article ke end mein aur Blog page par apne aap dikhne lagega.

> Dhyan do: AdSense ki kamai visitors par depend karti hai. Shuru mein kam traffic par kamai bhi kam hogi. Isliye lead generation (A) ko pehle rakho.

### C. Affiliate marketing
- Jo tools aap khud use karte ho (CRM, email tools, LinkedIn tools, Canva, hosting), unke **affiliate program** join karo.
- Article mein unka link do. Koi us link se kharide to aapko commission milta hai.
- Har tool ki website par neeche "Affiliate" ya "Partners" link hota hai. Hostinger, HubSpot aur Amazon Associates India jaise kai programs hain. Join karne se pehle unki terms padh lena.
- Article mein saaf likho ki yeh affiliate link hai. Yeh zaroori aur imaandar tareeka hai.

### D. Sponsored posts
- Jab blog par traffic aane lage, tab sales tools wali companies apne tool ke review ya mention ke liye paise deti hain.
- Contact page par likh do: "For sponsorships, email us".

### E. Digital products
- Jaise: "Cold email templates pack", "LinkedIn outreach playbook (PDF)" ya "ICP worksheet".
- Inhe Instamojo, Razorpay Payment Pages ya Gumroad par ₹199–₹999 mein bech sakte ho.

## 4. Google par blog ko upar kaise laayein (SEO)

1. Plugin install karo: **Plugins → Add New → "Rank Math SEO"** (ya Yoast SEO). Har article ke liye ek keyword chuno.
2. **Google Search Console** (search.google.com/search-console) mein site add karo aur sitemap submit karo. Rank Math sitemap khud bana deta hai.
3. **Site Kit by Google** plugin se visitors ki ginti dekho.
4. Har hafte **1–2 article** likho. Regular likhna bahut zaroori hai.
5. Har article **800+ words** ka rakho, aur usme 2–3 doosre articles ke links do.
6. Topic idea: jo sawaal clients call par poochte hain, wahi article ka title bana do.

| 4 | 2 naye article. 15+ articles ho jaayein to AdSense apply karo |
