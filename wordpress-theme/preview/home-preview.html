How it works

Four steps from cold contact to a meeting on your calendar.

You always know what we are doing, who we are reaching and what they said.

STEP 01

Know your buyer

We study your best clients and define exactly who to reach: industry, company size, role, and the moments when they are ready to buy.

STEP 02

Build the list

We research accounts by hand and verify every contact, so messages reach real decision-makers, not generic inboxes.

STEP 03

Start conversations

Personal outreach over email, LinkedIn and phone, written for each account. Every reply is handled by a person, not a bot.

STEP 04

Book and hand over

Interested buyers get a meeting on your calendar, with a short brief on who they are and what they care about.

Fit check

Good fit, or not yet?

We would rather tell you early than waste your time or budget.

A good fit if...

  • You sell to businesses, and a deal is worth a meeting
  • You know your ideal buyer, or want help defining one
  • Someone on your team can take calls and close
  • You want a steady flow of meetings, not a one-time spike

Not a fit if...

  • You want a bought database or mass email blasts
  • Your offer has no paying clients yet
  • No one is free to attend the meetings we book

FAQ

Most campaigns need two to three weeks to research, set up and start outreach. You get a realistic timeline after the strategy call.
